01 — Story

A cup is never
just a cup.

RAÍZ began in a tiled courtyard in Oaxaca, where our founders spent summers watching abuela grind beans by hand. What started as a reverence for ritual became a shop devoted to the craft — every bean traced, every pour considered.

We partner directly with highland farms, roast in small batches, and serve in a space built for the slow kind of morning.
